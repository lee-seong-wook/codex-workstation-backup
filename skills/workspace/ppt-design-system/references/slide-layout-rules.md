# slide-layout-rules.md

Layout rules and template anatomy. Use this together with `design-reference.md` (which holds tokens) to generate any slide.

All coordinates are on the **1280 × 720 px** reference canvas. They convert to python-pptx EMU via the `px()` helper in `SKILL.md`.

---

## 1. The fixed frame (every content slide)

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  KICKER · UPPERCASE                                          12 │  ← y=48
│                                                                    │
│  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────  │  ← y=88   (hairline)
│                                                                    │
│  Title sentence stating the insight                                │  ← y=104
│  Optional subtitle clarification                                   │  ← y=152
│                                                                    │
│  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐  │
│  │                                                              │  │
│  │                  CONTENT ZONE  (y = 200..664)                │  │
│  │                                                              │  │
│  │                                                              │  │
│  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  │
│                                                                    │
│  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────  │  ← y=672
│  SOURCE: …                                                         │  ← y=688
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Anchor coordinates table** — same on every content slide:

| Element | x | y | w | h |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kicker | 56 | 48 | 600 | 16 |
| Page number | 1124 | 48 | 100 | 16 |
| Top hairline | 56 | 88 | 1168 | 1 |
| Title | 56 | 104 | 1168 | 40 |
| Subtitle | 56 | 152 | 1168 | 24 |
| Content zone | 56 | 200 | 1168 | 464 |
| Bottom hairline | 56 | 672 | 1168 | 1 |
| Footer / source | 56 | 688 | 1168 | 16 |

The frame is generated by the `add_frame()` helper in `SKILL.md`. Templates only fill the **content zone**.

## 2. The 13 templates

Pick exactly one per slide. Same template ≤ 2 in a row.

### T1 — Cover

- No kicker (or very small deck name in MUTE).
- Title 44 pt at y=288 (vertical center).
- Sub 18 pt at y=344.
- Date / author 11 pt UPPERCASE at y=672.
- One full-bleed photo or one accent rule above title (1168 × 2 px ACCENT).

### T2 — Section divider

- Kicker = section number (`02 · DIAGNOSIS`).
- Title 36 pt at y=288.
- No subtitle. No content. No footer.
- Optional: vertical rule on far left (x=40, y=200..664, 1px).

### T3 — Agenda / contents

- Kicker `AGENDA`. Title `What we'll cover`.
- Content zone: numbered list.
  - Number column 56 px wide, 12 pt 600 ACCENT.
  - Item column rest, 14 pt 500 INK.
  - Vertical spacing 32 px between rows.
- Two columns if items > 5: split at midpoint.

### T4 — Statement

- Kicker, title-only frame.
- Content zone: one line of 36 pt 500 INK at y=312.
- One-line supporting note 14 pt MUTE at y=368.
- This is one of the **density-rule exceptions**.

### T5 — Hero number / KPI

- Title is the insight.
- Content zone left half (x=56, w=560): big number 56 pt 700 ACCENT, label 11 pt 600 UPPERCASE MUTE under it, change indicator next to label.
- Right half (x=648, w=576): up to 4 short framing bullets (12 pt body), one paragraph of context (12 pt MUTE) below.

### T6 — Two-column compare

- Title is the comparison statement.
- Content zone split: left 56..624 (w=568), right 656..1224 (w=568), 32 px gutter.
- Each column header: 11 pt 600 UPPERCASE MUTE, hairline under (1px, full column width).
- Each column body: 12 pt body, 12 pt vertical spacing.
- Use kickers like `OPTION A` / `OPTION B` — keep parallel structure.

### T7 — Three-column framework

- Three equal columns, gutter 24 px each: x=56/w=368, x=448/w=368, x=840/w=384.
- Each column: numbered marker (24×24 hairline circle), pillar title 14 pt 600, sub-points 12 pt 400.
- Optional one-line "so-what" footer below all three columns at y=624.

### T8 — Bullet narrative (workhorse)

- Left 2/3 (x=56, w=752): bullets.
  - 4–6 bullets, 12 pt body, 12 px vertical spacing.
  - Each bullet: lead phrase in 12 pt 600, supporting sub-line in 11 pt 400 MUTE optional.
- Right 1/3 (x=824, w=400): MUTE_FILL sidebar callout, padding 16 px. Header 11 pt 600 UPPERCASE, body 12 pt.

### T9 — Data, chart-focus

- Chart at left (x=56, y=200, w=720, h=440).
- Right column (x=800, w=424): "TAKEAWAY" label 11 pt UPPERCASE MUTE; insight sentence 14 pt 500 INK; 2–3 supporting bullets 12 pt 400 below.
- Chart source goes in the slide footer (y=688), not under the chart.

### T10 — Data, table-focus

- Table fills the content zone (x=56, y=216, w=1168, h=440).
- A "key takeaway" callout block above the table at y=200 height 16 (12 pt 600 INK, single line).

### T11 — Process / timeline

- Horizontal: 4–6 stages.
- Each stage: numbered marker on top, stage label 11 pt 600 UPPERCASE, 1–2 line description 12 pt below.
- Connector: 1 px hairline between markers, optional accent dot at the current step.
- All stages share the same y so the eye scans left to right.

### T12 — Quote

- Center-aligned quote 18 pt 500 INK, 480 px wide, max 4 lines, at y=240.
- Em-dash + attribution 11 pt UPPERCASE MUTE at y=400.
- Hairline above and below the quote, 320 px wide centered.
- Density-rule exception.

### T13 — Closing / next steps

- Title is the call to action.
- Numbered table inside content zone:
  - cols: # | Action | Owner | Date
  - 11 pt header row, 12 pt body, hairlines above/under header and bottom of table only.
- Bottom 80 px contains a contact line (email / contact person) in 12 pt MUTE.

## 3. Density rule (operational)

Run this check on every non-exception slide before saving:

1. Identify the lowest y-pixel reached by any content element (text bottom, chart bottom, table bottom).
2. If that y < **568**, the slide has too much empty lower space.
3. Add one of: footnote/source detail, sidebar callout, sub-bullet line, supporting micro-chart, "key takeaway" box.
4. Re-check.

**Exceptions** (intentional emptiness): T1 cover, T2 section divider, T4 statement, T12 quote.

## 4. HTML fallback (only when PPTX unavailable)

Same coordinates, same constraints, one `<section class="slide">` per slide.

```html
<!doctype html>
<html lang="ko">
<head>
<meta charset="utf-8">
<title>Deck</title>
<style>
  @import url("https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/orioncactus/pretendard/dist/web/static/pretendard.css");
  :root{
    --paper:#FAFAF7; --ink:#111111; --ink-soft:#1F1F1F; --mute:#4A4A4A;
    --submute:#6E6E6E; --hair:#E6E4DD; --fill:#F2F0EA; --accent:#1E3A8A;
  }
  html,body{margin:0;background:#222;font-family:"Pretendard",sans-serif;}
  .slide{
    position:relative; width:1280px; height:720px;
    background:var(--paper); margin:24px auto; box-shadow:0 1px 0 #000;
    color:var(--ink-soft); font-feature-settings:"tnum";
  }
  .kicker{position:absolute;left:56px;top:48px;font-size:11px;font-weight:600;
    letter-spacing:.12em;color:var(--submute);text-transform:uppercase;}
  .pageno{position:absolute;right:56px;top:48px;font-size:10px;font-weight:500;
    letter-spacing:.06em;color:#9A9A9A;}
  .rule-top, .rule-bot{position:absolute;left:56px;width:1168px;height:1px;background:var(--hair);}
  .rule-top{top:88px;} .rule-bot{top:672px;}
  .title{position:absolute;left:56px;top:104px;width:1168px;font-size:28px;
    font-weight:700;letter-spacing:-.01em;line-height:1.15;color:var(--ink);margin:0;}
  .subtitle{position:absolute;left:56px;top:152px;width:1168px;font-size:16px;
    font-weight:500;line-height:1.35;color:var(--mute);margin:0;}
  .footer{position:absolute;left:56px;top:688px;font-size:9px;font-weight:500;
    letter-spacing:.2em;color:#9A9A9A;text-transform:uppercase;}
  .content{position:absolute;left:56px;top:200px;width:1168px;height:464px;}
  .accent{color:var(--accent);}
  .sidebar{background:var(--fill);padding:16px;}
</style>
</head>
<body>

<section class="slide">
  <div class="kicker">01 · MARKET CONTEXT</div>
  <div class="pageno">04</div>
  <div class="rule-top"></div>
  <h1 class="title">Enterprise demand outpaced SMB for the third quarter running</h1>
  <p class="subtitle">Net new ARR was 71% enterprise, vs. a 58% Q1 baseline.</p>
  <div class="content">
    <!-- template-specific content here -->
  </div>
  <div class="rule-bot"></div>
  <div class="footer">SOURCE: INTERNAL FINANCE, Q3 2026 CLOSE</div>
</section>

</body>
</html>
```

## 6. Page numbering

- First counted page: cover = 1.
- Section dividers count.
- Render as zero-padded two-digit (`04`, `12`) on every content slide.
- Suppress page number on cover only (or render in MUTE smaller if the deck is long).

## 7. Common composition pitfalls

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Slide feels empty even with content | Content zone starts too low or ends too high | Verify y=200 start; check density rule (y_max ≥ 568) |
| "AI deck" feel | Template repetition | Vary across T5/T7/T8/T9 within a section |
| Title doesn't land | Title states topic, not insight | Rewrite per `writing-style.md` rule 1 |
| Too colorful | Accent used >3 times | Demote secondary accent uses to neutral grays |
| Charts look plasticky | 3D, shadows, gradient fills | Strip all to flat |
| Tables look like a wall | Vertical rules, no zebra | Drop vertical rules; consider zebra |
