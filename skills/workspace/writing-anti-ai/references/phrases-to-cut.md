# Phrases To Cut

Use this as a quick deletion list when rewriting text that sounds inflated, promotional, or evasive.

## English

Prefer deleting or replacing phrases like:

- "it is worth noting that"
- "it should be emphasized that"
- "plays a crucial role in"
- "serves as a testament to"
- "in today's rapidly evolving landscape"
- "underscores the importance of"
- "rich cultural heritage"
- "vibrant and dynamic"
- "deeply meaningful"
- "a wide range of"

Preferred rewrite pattern:

- Replace abstract praise with a concrete fact.
- Replace vague impact language with a measurable consequence.
- Replace scene-setting filler with the subject and verb.

## Chinese

Prefer deleting or replacing phrases like:

- "值得注意的是"
- "需要强调的是"
- "起着至关重要的作用"
- "充分体现了"
- "在快速发展的时代背景下"
- "彰显了其重要性"
- "具有深远意义"
- "丰富的文化底蕴"
- "生动展现了"
- "为...奠定了基础"

Preferred rewrite pattern:

- 用事实替代评价。
- 用具体动作替代抽象判断。
- 先写主体和动作，再补充必要背景。
